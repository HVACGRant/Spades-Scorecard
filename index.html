House rules
How scoring works
  • Partners: team bid = both partners' bids added (nil bids don't count toward it). Singles: everyone scores for themselves.
  • Make the bid: 10 × bid, plus 1 point per extra trick (a bag).
  • Miss the bid: lose 10 × bid.
  • Nil: win or lose the nil value (blind nil: the blind value) depending on taking zero tricks.
  • Every time bags reach the limit, lose the penalty and bags reset.
  • Game ends when someone reaches the target (or drops to the lose-at score). Highest score wins; a tie at the top plays on.
  • 2 players: draw-and-discard to 13 cards each, 13 tricks. 3 players: pull the 2♣, deal 17 each, 17 tricks.
